Security

Nothing to trust but the price.

NYES holds no funds between markets, runs no order book and has no admin who can pick a result.

01

Non-custodial.

Stakes sit in a per-market program account. They leave only as payouts, fees or refunds.

02

No resolver.

Results come from the Pyth TWAP. No key can override a settled market.

03

Fail to refund.

A stale or uncertain oracle voids the market and returns every stake with no fee.

Source Pyth price feedSettlement TWAP over the final 10 secondsPool cap 2% of onchain liquidity
